WoodTurningz

Role:   Social Media Content Creator  | Timeline: Oct. 2025 - Mar. 2026

Deliverables:

  • Social Media Content Creation
  • Social Media Marketing and Analytics
  • Email Marketing
  • Product Photography
  • Short-Form Video Editing and Production

    • Overview

    WoodTurningz is a woodworking and pen-turning supplier serving hobbyists, artisans, and professional makers. As a Social Media Content Creator, my role expanded beyond managing social channels into broader digital marketing, content production, and brand support. Working closely with the Art Director, I created visual content and marketing assets across social media, email, web, and video to strengthen the brand’s overall digital presence.

    Role

    As part of the marketing team, I was responsible for developing and producing digital content that supported both day-to-day marketing and larger campaign initiatives. The specific roles included:


  • Social media content creation and management (Facebook, Instagram, X).
  • Product photography and visual content production.
  • Video production and editing, including campaign content.  
  • Email marketing design in Stripo.
  • Website content updates and product imagery. 
  • Collaboration with the Art Director on campaign assets and brand consistency.

    • The Challenge

    WoodTurningz required a consistent flow of high-quality content to support ongoing marketing efforts across multiple platforms. The challenge was to create engaging visual assets that showcased products effectively while maintaining a cohesive brand identity across social media, email campaigns, website content, and promotional materials.


    WoodTurningz had previously struggled to maintain a consistent posting schedule, which had caused their social media engagement numbers to be extremely low in comparison to their number of followers. They sought out someone who could help increase their engagement numbers and in turn drive more sales through high-quality, consistent content.

    The Process and Tools

    Social Media Content

    I led the creation and publishing of content across Facebook, Instagram, and X, developing a consistent posting schedule and platform-specific approach to improve visibility and engagement.


    Content included product photography, promotional posts, and visual storytelling designed to highlight the craftsmanship of WoodTurningz customers.


    Performance and engagement were tracked using Hootsuite and Meta Business Suite analytics to evaluate content effectiveness and guide ongoing improvements.
