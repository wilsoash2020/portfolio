Star Wars - Photoshop Compositing

Personal Fan Art

A series of personal Photoshop composites created as creative exercises in photo manipulation and digital compositing. I combined multiple source images and assets to create new Star Wars-inspired posters, focusing on composition, masking, lighting, color, and visual effects.


My contribution:

  • Selected and combined source imagery
  • Created masks and selections
  • Built the compositions and backgrounds
  • Adjusted lighting, color, and atmosphere
  • Completed the final image editing

    • Final Composite Image

    Final image of Mandalorian poster announcing the release of season 3 of the TV show. The image was built through a combination of masking, layering, blending, color adjustments, lighting effects, and digital manipulation.

    The image above shows my photoshop file and workflow. Photoshop's layered workflow allowed me to compile multiple source images into a single cohesive image.
